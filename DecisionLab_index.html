2. Decision criteria

All results recalculate automatically when a payoff, name, or probability changes. Ties are retained.

Higher payoff = better

Maximax = highest possible payoff. Maximin = highest worst-case payoff. Minimax Regret = lowest maximum opportunity loss. EMV / Bayes = highest probability-weighted expected payoff.

3. Staged filtering

Each stage keeps every alternative tied for the best score on that criterion among the alternatives still remaining.
